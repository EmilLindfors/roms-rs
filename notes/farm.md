# Salmon-farm application target and cage drag (F.1)

## Application target: currents around a salmon farm

Goal (added 2026-09-26): resolve currents at a farm site (cages ≈ 40–60 m across, nets 20–40 m deep) inside a NorKyst-driven coastal domain, and track sea-lice larvae, feed and faeces from the cages. A 3D visualisation (Bevy, separate crate) reads the model output; it is not part of `dg-rs`.

This section only orders the existing items for that goal and adds the two farm-specific ones (F.1, F.2). Everything else lives in its own priority section.

**Stage A: 2D farm-scale currents.** Depth-averaged, so it answers tidal exposure and the wake of the farm, not the surface layer.
1. P1.3 geometry (blocker): coastline-fitted meshes with elements of tens of metres at the farm and kilometres offshore. General quadrilaterals are supported in 2D since 2026-09-26, and all-quad Gmsh meshes (MSH 4.1/2.2) load since 2026-09-26 (see `docs/gmsh-meshes.md`). A coastline-fitted Frøya mesh is built from the topobathy model since 2026-09-27 (`scripts/gmsh_coastline_mesh.py`, `froya_real_data mesh=`); since 2026-09-28 without the degenerate quads that set its time step (1.84× faster), it costs ≈ 4× the 500 m grid per model hour, mostly in the local time stepping's stage dependency (see P1.3). Triangles or quad-dominant meshes and CSR are still open.
2. P1.6 shoreline cliffs and bathymetry: done at 50 m (2026-09-26: `BedRaster`, `Bathymetry2D::project`, Kartverket's topobathy model, `raise_isolated_wet_nodes`). Farm-scale meshes (tens of metres) need the service's 1 m level, which has depths only inside surveyed projects: merge it over the 50 m model where it has data (see P1.6).
3. ~~F.1 cage drag (2D form)~~ done 2026-09-26 (`CageDrag2D`, `with_cage_drag`).
4. ~~P2.5 local time stepping~~ done 2026-09-27 (`MultirateSSPRK3`: 2.4–2.8× on a 20 m farm mesh; with horizontal viscosity since 2026-09-28; see P2.5 for the follow-ups).
5. ~~P1.5 nesting of NorKyst ū, v̄, η and the P1.6 atmospheric forcing reader~~ done 2026-09-27 (`OceanModelState` with transport scaling, relaxation band and bed blending; `AtmosphereReader` + `GriddedAtmosphere2D`; gauge-corrected tides with NorKyst's residual, `with_tidal_correction`; see P1.5 for the follow-ups).
6. ~~P3.1 current validation~~ tooling done 2026-09-27: station series of η, ū, v̄ sampled at the station by evaluating the DG polynomial there (`PointLocator2D`, `Probe2D`; F.2 reuses them), tidal-ellipse fit and complex difference (`fit_tidal_ellipses`), ADCP comparison paired by time. Still to do: real current data (farm-site surveys, see P3.1).
7. ~~F.2 particle tracking in 2D~~ done 2026-09-28 (`particles::ParticleTracker2D`: RK4 on the DG velocity, random walk, walls, open exits, stranding; online or between snapshots; see F.2 for the follow-ups).
8. ~~Minor: `SpatiallyVaryingManning2D` as `BottomFriction2D` (P1.2); the vacuous tests in P1.7~~ done 2026-09-28.

### F.1 Cage drag
- [x] Momentum sink from the net as a porous region: S = −½·C_d·a·|u|u per unit volume, with a (net area per volume) and C_d from the net solidity (Løland 1991; review in Klebert et al. 2013, Ocean Eng. 58). Fouling raises the solidity, so it is a per-cage parameter (`NetCage`, `NetCage::circular` with Løland's C_d and a = 4/(πR)).
  - [x] 2D (2026-09-26, `source/swe_2d/cage.rs`): integrated over the net depth only, Λ = ½C_d a |u| min(d_net, h)/h, over the cage footprint.
  - [x] 3D (2026-10-01, `physics::cage_drag`, `Hydrostatic3D::with_cage_drag`): per σ-layer, weighted by the layer's share above the net bottom; linearised like the 3D bottom drag (`−Λ̄ū` point-implicit in the barotropic pass, the shear part in `G`, `−λ_l u_l` implicit in the vertical solve). Gates in `simulation_3d.rs`: unsheared flow decays as the 2D drag (first order), only the layers inside the net slow down, wind against nets reaches `Σ H_l λ_l u_l = τ/ρ₀` to 8.7e-14.
    - [ ] `G`'s shear part `−D Σ Δσ_l λ_l (u_l − ū)` is explicit (AB3-extrapolated with the rest of `G`): at `λΔt ≥ 10` it overshoots and reverses the stopped flow by < 1 % of `u₀` (`a_strong_cage_drag_is_stable_at_long_steps`; none up to 5). Harmless at farm steps (`λ ≈ 0.005–0.02 /s` at 0.5–1 m/s, Δt ≲ 60 s), but a coarse 3D run with long baroclinic steps through a farm should keep `λΔt` below ≈ 5. The bottom drag's shear part has the same structure. Measured 2026-10-01: not the AB3 extrapolation (with `G` frozen at `tⁿ` the numbers are identical; the first step, AB1, already overshoots). It is the split itself: the pass damps the whole `ū` at `Λ̄` while the drag acts only on the netted layers. Any implicit rate `Λ_imp ≥ 0` in the pass, with `G` taking `D Σ Δσ_l λ_l u_l − Λ_imp ū` at `tⁿ`, keeps the steady balance exact; the drag-weighted mean `Λ_imp = Σ a_l λ_l (u_l·D̃)/|D̃|²`, `a_l = Δσ_l λ_l/(1 + Δtλ_l)`, `D̃ = Σ a_l u_l`, makes one pass equal the vertical solve's backward-Euler mean when nothing else acts, but the pass's per-stage implicit damping compounds to ≈ `e^{−ΛΔt}`, not `1/(1 + ΛΔt)`, so the match needs `G` and the rate chosen against that (and `dt` in `G`, which `slow_forcing_into` does not get). Not worth it at farm steps.
    - [ ] Wake turbulence: the nets produce TKE (`P = Σ λ_l |u_l|²` per unit mass, as in canopy models; Beudin et al. 2017 for vegetation in ROMS/COAWST) that GLS does not see; the wake's mixing below the cages comes only from the shear the drag makes. Since 2026-10-01 `k` and `ψ` are advected (P4.4), so net-made TKE would be carried into the wake: add it as a source of the `k` equation (and `ψ`'s, with Beudin's `c_ψ4`) per caged layer.
    - [x] A 3D farm run: `examples/farm_3d.rs` (2026-10-01), two cages in a stratified, periodic tidal channel, 3D against 2D with and without cages, the profile behind a cage, the drag force, and particles (larvae, faeces, feed) from the cages. At 3 h (≈ 0.34 m/s):
      - The depth-mean wake is the same in 3D and 2D (10.0 % deficit 50 m behind the cage in both; 5.1 against 4.4 % at 400 m), but in 3D it is a 20 % deficit over the net's 20 m, unchanged 100 m behind, with 4–12 % more current underneath.
      - The drag on the farm is 90 kN in 3D against 72 kN in 2D: the current is surface-intensified (0.37 m/s in the top layers, 0.34 depth mean) and the nets hang in the fast part. A depth-averaged model underestimates cage loads by ≈ 20 % here.
      - Feed (10 cm/s) lands ≈ 110 m down-current, faeces (3 cm/s) ≈ 290 m.
      - Larvae released over the top 5 m stay there (passive, `lice=passive`: 10/50/90 % at 0.5/2.3/4.2 m after 1 h; swimming, the default `lice=ladim` since 2026-10-01: 0.0/0.6/2.4 m, all swimming up in the 03:00 UTC June light): with no wind and a 2 °C thermocline at 10 m, GLS leaves K ≈ 1e-6 m²/s above it (1e-4 at 15 m, 2–3e-2 in the bottom boundary layer). Real sites need the wind (and the surface-wave TKE item in P4.4) before larval depth statistics mean anything. Since 2026-10-01 `farm_3d wind= waves= closure=` has both: generic GLS under 0.1 Pa gives K ≈ 2e-3 m²/s at 1 m (log-layer surface) or 1e-2 (breaking waves, z₀ₛ = 1 m), and the larvae spread to 0.4/2.7/6.1 m or 0.8/3.4/7.2 m; real sites still need the wind and `H_s` from forcing files (P4.4).
      - With `k`, `ψ` advected (P4.4, 2026-10-01) the farm is unchanged to the reported digits: wake 10.02 % at 50 m and 5.03 % at 400 m, drag 89.9 kN. At 0.34 m/s the channel's turbulence is made and dissipated where it is; advection should matter at fronts, sills and in wind-mixed layers carried into still water, not here.
      - The real fjord site with a tide through an open boundary: done in 2D 2026-10-24 (Kattholmen, below). The 2D and 3D undisturbed currents differ (0.31 against 0.34 m/s) because Chézy on ū is not the 3D log-layer drag on the bottom layer (the P4.4 friction-consistency item).
    - [ ] The splitter's layer-drag buffer is dense (`[element][node][level]`, `ModeSplitIntegrator` `layer_drag_rate`) and `Hydrostatic3D::layer_drag_into` zeroes all of it every step, though only the cage nodes are ever nonzero. Cheap next to a 3D step; a sparse form over `CageDrag2D::nodes` (rates per caged column) would also let the vertical solve skip the lookup.
  - [x] Cages are comparable to or smaller than an element: each node is weighted by the area of its GLL subcell inside the footprint over w·J (adaptive bisection on the signed distance), so Σ wJφ is the footprint area on any quadrilateral.
- [x] Point-implicit with the bottom friction (`ImplicitDamping2D::cages`, `SWEPhysics2DBuilder::with_cage_drag`).
- [x] Gate tests (`tests/cage_drag_test.rs`):
  - Porous band across a periodic channel driven by a body force: momentum-flux and pressure loss across the band = drag − forcing (1e-3); total drag = total forcing (1e-4).
  - Mass conserved; lake at rest over a rough bed with cages exact (1e-10).
  - Magnitude: domain-wide cage against a body force reaches the analytic u² = 2Gh/(C_d a min(d, h)) (1e-6); decay of uniform flow converges at first order.
- [x] A farm-wake case at a real site (2026-10-24): Kattholmen (site 14042) in the Mausund box, `froya_real_data … site=data/sites/14042.txt` (the command is in the example's docs; the analysis is `examples/common/farm_site.rs`, the maps `scripts/farm_wake_map.py`).
  - Setup: 2D, nested in NorKyst with the atlas tides, on the 3D-smoothed bed (`slopes3d=on debug_3d=thin=1`, which the island-lee validation needs), P2, LTS 8, ν = 1 m²/s, 31 h from 2025-06-15 00 UTC with a 3 h ramp. The site's 8 cages (`CageGrid::RING_160M`: 2 × 4, radius 25.5 m, nets 20 m, solidity 0.25) as `CageDrag2D`, against the same tide without them. 275 elements of 11–29 m edges within 170 m of the farm's centre.
  - Cost: 5385 s wall without the cages, 5155 s with them (≈ 170 s per model hour; the machine was shared with builds and tests part of the time). Particle tracking took 84 s of it.
  - Results over the last 2 M2 periods (6.16–31 h):
    - The farm lies in slack water: 0.039 m/s mean at its centre, largest 0.10. NorKyst has 0.21–0.25 m/s M2 here (the island lee, P3.1), so every number below scales with a current that may be 2–5× too weak. The principal axis is 121°/301°, with 0.015 m/s RMS across it. The residual is 0.017 m/s toward 146°: the farm is on the edge of an eddy south of a 0.10–0.15 m/s westward residual stream 300–600 m to the north.
    - The cages slow the farm by 9 %: speeds at the cage centres are 0.89–0.94 of those without cages (farm mean 0.041 against 0.045 m/s).
    - Drag on the farm: mean 5.8 kN, largest 15.5 kN at 0.086 m/s. That is 1.9 kN per cage; scaled by u² to 0.34 m/s it is ≈ 30 kN per cage, close to `farm_3d`'s 36 kN in 2D.
    - The wake is short. Along the current through the centre the deficit is 5–8 % inside the farm (the farm's edge is 136–144 m from the centre), 2.2 % (toward 138°) and 0.6 % (toward 289°) at 200 m, i.e. 60 m beyond the edge, and about 1 % or less from 300 m on. Samples with ≥ 0.02 m/s at the centre: 97 toward 138°, 31 toward 289°.
    - Wake area over 5 / 10 / 20 %: 12.7 / 2.6 / 0.2 ha toward 138° and 8.6 / 2.2 / 0.2 ha toward 289° (the frame itself is 6.6 ha).
    - Residual: at the centre 0.0165 m/s toward 146° without the cages, 0.0154 m/s toward 144° with them. The cages change it by 1.9 mm/s on average within 250 m and 0.7 mm/s within 1 km. The largest change, 4.7 mm/s, is 130 m north-north-east of the centre, where the cages push the eddy north-east. That is a 10–20 % change of a 2–4 cm/s residual near the farm, and nothing beyond 1 km.
    - Particles: the depth drift's effect on connectivity is in `notes/particles.md` (F.2, the depth drift).

At ν = 1 the farm-scale Péclet number is UL/ν ≈ 0.04 · 100 / 1 ≈ 4, so viscosity, not the cages, shapes the wake; at 0.1 it is ≈ 80. The physical estimates are lower still: Elder's depth-mean 0.07 u* h ≈ 0.004–0.01 m²/s (u* = 0.002–0.005 m/s, h = 30 m), Okubo's (1971) diffusivity at 100 m, 0.0103 L^1.15 cm²/s ≈ 0.04 m²/s.
