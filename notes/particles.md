# Lagrangian particle tracking (F.2)

### F.2 Lagrangian particle tracking
- [x] Point location: find the element holding a point and its reference coordinates (2026-09-27, P3.1: `mesh::PointLocator2D`, a bucket grid over element bounding boxes and Newton on the bilinear map). Curved (isoparametric) elements, once P1.3 has them, need the same Newton on the higher-order map.
- [x] 2D tracking (2026-09-28, `src/particles/`): `ParticleTracker2D` moves `Particle2D`s through a `ParticleVelocity2D`, sampled as the element polynomial at the particle (`interpolation_weights_into` on the stack, no allocation).
  - [x] Velocity: `SWEVelocity2D` is (hu, hv)/h of a solution, steady or linear in time between two snapshots (output files, or online: the previous and current state a `Simulation` callback hands over); `NodalVelocity2D` is any nodal field.
  - [x] Locating: every move walks the straight segment face by face from the particle's element (`particles::walk`), O(faces crossed), no bucket search after release.
  - [x] RK4 advection; horizontal random walk √(2KΔt)ξ with constant K. Each particle has its own SplitMix64 stream (tracker seed, particle id), so a run does not depend on thread count or particle order. Particles are stepped in parallel with `parallel`.
  - [x] Boundaries: walls reflect specularly (`with_reflecting` picks the tags; untagged faces reflect), other faces let the particle out at the crossing (`ParticleStatus::Exited(tag)`), periodic faces carry it across. Particles in water shallower than `with_stranding_depth` strand and float again when it deepens.
  - [x] Gates (`tests/particle_tracking_test.rs`):
    - Solid-body rotation on distorted quads (exact in Q1): one revolution returns to 7e-7 at 100 steps, rate 4.00.
    - A walk of 0.45 per step (several elements) with a flow into a corner never leaves the basin.
    - Well-mixed: a uniform distribution stays uniform in a closed basin (χ² over 64 bins 72 and 57 for steps below and above the element size); in open water the variance is 2Kt to 5 %.
    - Open exit at the crossing point, periodic wrap, stranding and refloating, reproducibility per particle.
    - Online in a running `Simulation` (uniform oscillating flow): second order in the snapshot interval (rate 2.00).
  - [x] `local_time_stepping_farm particles=N [particle_seconds=60] [kh=0.1]` releases N particles in each cage and tracks them online. 1 h from rest with 500 per cage (4 threads): the tracking costs 0.08 s against 198 s for the solver. The clouds drift 42 m with the flood and spread 38 m RMS, which is the random walk's √(4Kt) (the tidal current at the farm is mm/s, so the site says little about advection; a farm-wake study needs a site with real currents, see F.1).

- [x] Pen-to-pen connectivity statistics (2026-10-03, `particles::connectivity`). Contact zones (`ContactZone`: a circular footprint over a depth range). `ConnectivityRecorder` adds up each particle's weighted time in each zone after every tracking step (rectangle rule, `|ΔE| ≤ Δt` per passage). It counts a particle's own zone only after the particle has been `with_return_distance` beyond it. `ConnectivityMatrix` gives share, mean exposure and binomial error per (source, receiver) for any set of release groups; `contacts` gives each contact's exposure and age at first contact; `ConnectivityEnsemble` gives the spread across seeds, standard errors and the `Difference` of two ensembles.
  - Gates (`tests/particle_connectivity_test.rs`):
    - In a uniform current, every particle's exposure equals its chord time through the next cage to the step (3.92 s at Δt = 4 s, 0.95 s at 1 s).
    - A random walk's mean occupation time of a disc around its release matches the exact `Σ Δt (1 − e^{−a²/4Kt_n})` (191.7 ± 1.6 s against 192.5 s).
    - Independent particles: the spread across 40 seeds is the binomial error (0.027 against 0.028). The same layout with other seeds differs by z = 1.0, a receiver 15 m further away by z = 21.6.
  - `farm_3d particles=N layout=across|along releases= release_every= seeds=R contact_depth= connectivity=<csv> runs=cages`: larvae in R seeds (extra seeds track the larvae alone). For each batch it reports the release time, M2 phase and current at the farm, then per pair the share (mean ± spread, binomial), exposure per larva, the 10/50/90 % exposure among the larvae in contact, and the median age at first contact. The CSV has every seed's matrix per batch. The 9 h runs below use 200 per cage, 4 hourly batches from 2 h and 8 seeds, ≈ 650 s wall each, of which 10 s is tracking.
    - Across the flow (120 m apart): 4.3 ± 0.7 % from cage 0 to cage 1 and 2.8 ± 0.6 % back, all on the ebb, after 3–6 h. Each cage's own larvae return at 39 / 38 %: 28 % for the batch released at 2 h (back after 6.5 h), 59 % for the one near slack (back after 68 min). The channel's tide is reversible with no residual current, so the ebb brings the cloud back over the farm; a real site needs its residual current (item 3 of the plan).
    - Along the flow: 88.0 ± 0.8 % from cage 0 to cage 1 on the flood (first contact at 7 min, median exposure 225 s) and 36.4 ± 1.1 % back on the ebb. Released near slack, the larvae linger: 896 s of exposure per larva from 0 to 1.
    - The two layouts' own-cage return from cage 0 differs by 3.2 ± 0.5 % (39.2 against 36.0 %, z ≈ 6), so a few-percent layout effect is measurable with 8 seeds of 800 larvae per cage.
    - Edge flicker: without a return distance, the walk stepping out of a cage and back gave a spurious 2–3 % own-cage contact at 1 min (one step of exposure). `farm_3d` uses a cage radius.
  - [ ] The spread across seeds is about half the binomial error (0.9 against 1.7 % over all batches). The sunflower release points are the same in every seed, so the spread measures the walk's noise in one flow and one release. That is the right yardstick for comparing layouts or `kh` in the same flow; it is not the uncertainty of the release. Draw the release points per seed (and per batch) for that.
  - [ ] `farm_3d`'s periodic channel is 6 km long. The peak-to-peak tidal excursion `2U/ω` = 7.1 km, so larvae released at slack can meet the farm's periodic image. In the 4.5 h run the larvae's mean drift was 2.2 km (RMS spread 0.8 km) at 2.5 h after release, still on the flood. The 9 h runs' largest excursion was not logged: log the larvae's largest displacement before running longer or releasing at slack.
  - [ ] Zone lookup is O(particles × zones) per step (`ConnectivityRecorder::record` tests every footprint). That is fine for one site; for region-wide farm registers (hundreds of sites) use a bucket grid over the zones' boxes, as `PointLocator2D` does for elements.
  - [ ] Recording is serial after each parallel step (`FarmParticles::steps`). Records are independent per particle, so it parallelises the same way when 10⁵+ particles make it count. It was not measurable here: 10 s of tracking for 4 × 9600 larvae-seeds plus 9600 faeces and feed over 9 h.
  - [ ] Exposure is first order in the step. The exact passage time within a step (segment–circle intersection, linear in the step) would make it second order, if `UΔt` cannot be kept small against the cage.
  - [ ] Only circular footprints. Site polygons (Fiskeridirektoratet's register, F.1) and a whole-farm zone around a cage group would need polygon zones (a point-in-polygon test behind `ContactZone::covers`).
  - [ ] The matrix splits by release group only. Contact time split by the tidal phase at contact (not release) needs the contacts' times, which `Contact` keeps only as `first`. Add per-phase exposure bins if that question comes up.
- [x] Sensitivity of the connectivity to the horizontal diffusivity (2026-10-03). `farm_3d kh=0.1,0.01,0.03,0.3,1` tracks the larvae in every `K` in one run, the flow being the same (8 seeds each; 45 s of tracking in a 650 s run; the CSV gains a `kh` column). Shares over all 4 batches, mean ± standard error of 8 seeds:

  | K (m²/s) | across 0→0 | across 0→1 | along 0→1 (flood) | along 1→0 (ebb) |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 0.01 | 73.3 ± 0.7 % | 0.0 % | 94.8 ± 0.3 % | 72.5 ± 0.3 % |
  | 0.03 | 57.9 ± 0.4 % | 0.3 ± 0.1 % | 93.1 ± 0.3 % | 54.9 ± 0.4 % |
  | 0.1 | 39.2 ± 0.4 % | 4.3 ± 0.3 % | 88.0 ± 0.3 % | 36.4 ± 0.4 % |
  | 0.3 | 26.2 ± 0.5 % | 9.5 ± 0.5 % | 79.8 ± 0.4 % | 23.5 ± 0.4 % |
  | 1 | 19.0 ± 0.5 % | 11.9 ± 0.4 % | 67.8 ± 0.6 % | 16.0 ± 0.5 % |

  - Over 0.01–1 m²/s, `K` moves every share by far more than the noise (z 9–73 against `K` = 0.1). For the return on the ebb and the cross-flow neighbour it moves them more than the layout does: the cross-flow share goes from none to 12 %, and the return to the own cage from 73 to 19 %. The connectivity at the scale of the pens is set by `K`, so `K` needs a physical basis and a site's own measurements (drifter or dye spreading) before the numbers mean much.
  - For scale: Okubo's (1971) diagram gives `K ≈ 0.0103 ℓ^1.15` cm²/s, 0.01 m²/s at ℓ = 30 m and 0.04 m²/s at 100 m. The default 0.1 is 3–10× that at the cages' scale. The model's own `nu=1` background is a numerical choice and 10–100× it.
- [x] A horizontal diffusivity that varies with the flow (2026-10-03, `particles::diffusivity`). `HorizontalDiffusivity` builds a `HorizontalDiffusivityField` from any nodal `K` (one level or σ-levels). Nodes that coincide across faces, periodic ones included (union-find over the face-node pairs), take their mean, so `K` is continuous and `∇K` is the element polynomial's gradient. `smagorinsky_2d`/`smagorinsky_3d` give `ν/Pr_t` with `ν = ν₀ + (C_s Δ)²|S|` from the depth-mean or each layer's strain. `ParticleVelocity2D`/`3D::horizontal_diffusivity` is the hook (forwarded by the Stokes wrappers). `WithHorizontalDiffusivity2D`/`3D` add a field to any flow, steady or linear in time between two (`DiffusivityInTime`). The trackers step `x += ∇K Δt + √(2(K_tracker + K_field)Δt) ξ` (Euler–Maruyama at the step's start).
  - Gates: `tests/particle_diffusivity_test.rs`. Visser's well-mixed test in 2D with `K` varying tenfold on distorted P2 quads: χ² over 64 bins 74.9 with the drift, 9540 without. In 3D the field's `K` at σ = −0.5 between two levels plus the tracker's constant spreads at `2Kt` to 1.3 %. Unit tests: continuity across faces, an exact gradient for a field in the elements' space, Smagorinsky's `K` of a linear shear.
  - `farm_3d kh=smag` (Smagorinsky's `K` of each layer's strain, the model's `cs`, `Pr_t` = 1, without the numerical `nu`; `smag+0.01` adds a constant). Each callback builds the field from the state, linear in time between two. The report gives `K_h` up-current, in the lee and over the top layer.
    - At `dx=60` P2 (Δ = 30 m, `cs` 0.2), at the end of the 9 h run across the flow, Smagorinsky's `K_h` over the top layer is 0.011 / 0.029 / 0.18 m²/s (50 / 90 / 100 %), and 0.005–0.008 100 m behind cage 0. That is Okubo's value for the scale, and an order below the default 0.1.
    - Across the flow, over all batches (z against `K` = 0.1): `smag` returns 82.5 ± 0.6 % of cage 0's larvae to it (z +62) and sends none to the other cage; `smag+0.01` 69.3 %, none; `smag+0.03` 56.8 %, 0.5 %. `smag` keeps more at home than a constant 0.01 (73.3 %) although its median is 0.011. Why is not established: the report samples `K` at two points at the end of the run only. Log `K` along the larvae's paths (mean `K` seen per larva) to tell.
    - Along the flow: `K_h` is 0.15 m²/s 100 m east of cage 0 (between the cages, in the wake's strain) at the end of the run, 0.0067 / 0.027 / 0.18 over the top layer. `smag` sends 92.8 ± 0.2 % from cage 0 to cage 1 and returns 80.0 ± 0.4 % from 1 to 0 on the ebb (88.0 / 36.4 % at 0.1); `smag+0.01` 92.1 / 66.3 %, `smag+0.03` 90.8 / 53.1 %.
    - So with the model's own closure the pens are more tightly connected to themselves and to their neighbours along the tide than with the default 0.1, and not at all across it. The 0.01 m²/s background is what most moves the result (82.5 → 69.3 % return across), and nothing on this mesh constrains it.
  - [ ] Euler–Maruyama with `K` at the step's start is weak order 1. Where `K` varies over a walk step (`√(2KΔt)` against `K/|∇K|`, e.g. at the edge of a wake), Visser's half-drift-step evaluation or the backward Itô scheme (Spivakovskaya et al. 2007) is better. The 2D gate passes at Δt = 0.5 s; measure the error against Δt before using large steps.
  - [ ] Smagorinsky's `K` follows the model's resolved strain. It is a closure for the unresolved scales, so it falls as the mesh refines (`Δ²`) while the resolved eddies take over the spreading. Check that connectivity converges with mesh refinement at the pens (plan item "smaller items").
- [x] 3D tracking (2026-10-01, `particles::ParticleTracker3D`, `Solution3DVelocity`): a σ-level per particle, RK4 on (x, y, σ) with `dσ/dt = Ω/D + w_p/D` (the 2D tracker's walk, walls, exits and stranding horizontally); `u, v` linear in σ between the layer centres, Ω from `Solution3D::w` with Ω = 0 at the surface and bed; snapshots linear in time.
  - [x] Vertical random walk with the ∂K/∂z drift correction (Visser 1997, MEPS 158) on the model's `eddy_diffusivity` at the w-points (`with_vertical_random_walk`). Gates (`tests/particle_tracking_3d_test.rs`): Visser's well-mixed test, χ² over 20 bins 7.3 with the drift against 417 without (analytic K, `K' ≠ 0` at the surface), and 5.6 against 5435 on a `Solution3D` holding a log-layer K at 20 w-points (piecewise linear, `K'` jumping at every w-point); a sheared flow with a σ-velocity followed exactly; sinking particles reach the bed at `D/w_s` and settle (`with_bed_settling`, `ParticleStatus::Settled`) or reflect.
  - [x] Sinking speed (feed, faeces): a constant vertical speed per particle (`release(.., vertical_speed)`).
  - [x] Lice larvae depth preference and salinity avoidance (2026-10-01, `particles::behaviour`): `ParticleBehaviour3D` gives each particle a swimming speed per step from what it senses (`Surroundings`: depth, T and S through the new `ParticleVelocity3D::tracers`, sampled from `Solution3D` like `u`), accrues development and kills it (`ParticleStatus::Dead`); `ParticleTracker3D::step_with`. `SalmonLice` is the IMR lice model's larva: up at 0.5 mm/s where the light `E₀e^{−0.2ζ}` reaches its stage's threshold, down where S is below its threshold (wins over light), nauplius → copepodid → dead in degree-days, survival weight `e^{−0.17 t/day}`. Presets `ladim()` (the operational LADiM `salmon_lice` IBM: light 0.01, S < U[30, 32] nauplii / U[20, 28] copepodids, 40/170 degree-days, T ≥ 5 °C) and `johnsen_2014()` (Johnsen et al. 2014, AEI 5: light 0.39 / 2.06e-5 µmol m⁻² s⁻¹, S < 20, 50/150 degree-days). `ClearSkyLight`: Skartveit & Olseth's clear sky at a site, as LADiM computes it (equal to LADiM's code to 1e-9 at whole hours).
    - Gates (`tests/particle_tracking_3d_test.rs`): under constant K, light-seeking larvae reach `c ∝ e^{−wζ/K}` above the threshold depth and uniform below (χ² 23.4 over 20 bins with 20 000 particles; 64 and 56 with the swim 5 % off), and a halocline traps them in `c ∝ e^{−w|ζ−ζ_h|/K}` (χ² 11.4); development and death to the step from a two-temperature `Solution3D`.
    - [ ] The swim is chosen at the start of each step (as LADiM), so a cue's jump is smeared over a walk step `√(2KΔt)`: the light gate's χ² is 13 / 23 / 41 at Δt = 5 / 10 / 20 s with K = 5e-3 m²/s (weak order ½ for a discontinuous drift). Keep `√(2KΔt)` well below the profile scale `K/w_s` near haloclines, or locate the crossing within the step.
    - [ ] Infectivity: LADiM weights copepodids by Skern-Mauritzen et al.'s (2020) infectivity(age, T) for infection-pressure maps; add it next to `SalmonLice::survival`. The connectivity recorder takes it as the contact weight (`ConnectivityRecorder::record(.., weight)`; `farm_3d` passes 1).
    - [ ] Light: clear sky at one site only. Use the weather readers' shortwave or cloud cover (`AtmosphereReader`) and an attenuation tied to salinity / CDOM (Aksnes et al. 2009) for fjords; a per-position sun through the mesh projection for domains wider than ≈ 1° of longitude.
    - [ ] A behaviour sees the development, not the particle, so a mixed population steps one slice per species (`farm_3d` keeps one `Vec` per kind). Pass `&Particle3D` (id, vertical speed) if per-particle traits are needed.
  - [ ] Ω at the layer centres is an average of the w-point values (`Solution3D::w`, output only); the tracker interpolates it with Ω = 0 at the ends. Keeping Ω at the w-points in the state (as the turbulence and now `eddy_diffusivity` are) would make `dσ/dt` exact to the model's continuity.
  - [ ] Stranding in 3D uses the column depth only; resuspension of settled particles (bed stress above a critical value) is not modelled.
  - [ ] Sampling cost: `Solution3DVelocity::diffusivity` runs the whole `blend` (u, v, Ω, depth and K of both snapshots) to return K, and the Visser walk calls it twice per step on top of the four RK stages' `velocity` calls (each also sampling K it discards). Split `Sample` by what the caller needs, and bracket σ once per call; measure on `farm_3d particles=N` first (tracking 1200 particles costs 0.6 s of a 163 s 3D run, so it matters only for 10⁵+ particles).
