# 3D: vertical mixing and boundary layers (P4.4)

### P4.4 Vertical mixing and boundary layers
- [x] GLS k-ε (NorKyst's closure) as a per-column solve reusing the tridiagonal solver (Umlauf & Burchard 2003; Warner et al. 2005). Done 2026-10-01 (`physics::GlsMixing`, module `physics::gls`): k-ε, k-ω and Umlauf & Burchard's generic model with Canuto A/B quasi-equilibrium stability functions, following GOTM (`tkeeq`, `genericeq`, `cmue_d`): `k`, `ψ` at the w-points in `Solution3D::tke`/`gls`, one backward-Euler step per baroclinic step before the implicit vertical diffusion (Patankar sinks, log-layer flux conditions, Galperin's length limit), `c₃⁻` from Ri_st = 0.25, κ from log-layer consistency, `c₃⁺` from k-ε's `c₃ε⁺ = 1`.
  - Gates: Kato–Phillips entrainment within 5 % of Price's `1.05 u*√(t/N₀)` at 12–30 h for all presets (k-ε 1.004–1.014, independent of Δt 10/60 s); open channel: bottom stress u*², `k` in local equilibrium to 3 %, the wall length `κz` at the first w-point (0.96 at 80 levels); homogeneous shear turbulence steady at Ri_st; an unstable column overturns in < 3 h; the 3D step bit-identical on 1 and 4 threads with GLS.
  - Found on the way: ROMS's `c₃⁺ = 1` for every model makes k-ω's implied ε equation `c₃ε⁺ = 2`, and k-ω then never started convection from weak turbulence (0.45 of a 0.5 °C inversion left after 12 h). The presets use GOTM's `(3/2 − c₃ε⁺)n + m` (k-ω 0, generic 0.665).
  - [x] Advect `k` and `ψ` (ROMS does; GOTM, being 1D, does not): horizontally with the layer transports and vertically with Ω, both at the w-points. Matters where turbulence is carried faster than it decays: tidal fronts and the lee of sills, the farm wake. Done 2026-10-01 (`Hydrostatic3D::with_turbulence_advection`, on by default with limited Akima vertically): the splitter carries `tke`/`gls` through the 3D stages as inventories over the w-cells (half the end layers at the bed and surface, `w_cell_thicknesses`), advected by the layer transports averaged to them (`LayerTransport::stagger_from`), so constancy and conservation are the tracers'; the closure then steps the sources in each column (operator splitting, as ROMS).
    - Gates: uniform `k` under a tide over a slope 2.5e-14 (with a river too), `ψ`'s inventory 3.4e-15; the wet/dry beach 1.7e-9 (interior `k`) and 7.9e-11 (`ψ` inventory); a turbulence patch in a 0.5 m/s current moves 900.01 m in 30 min (U·t = 900), stays put without advection. Cost: +7 % of the 3D step with GLS (`profile_3d mixing=gls turbulence=local|advected`: transport RHS 16 → 25 ms at 1024 P2 × 20 levels).
    - Found on the way: the end w-points hold the closure's boundary values (the wall's `l = κz₀`), not half-layer means; advected at their own values they cut the top viscosity to the background wherever Ω pointed down and decoupled the surface layer (`profile_3d` surface current 0.28 against 0.175 m/s after 20 steps). They are advected at their neighbours' values now. And a non-positive `ψ` from an undershoot made the closure reset the whole column (NaN through the implicit solve, then the final `max`); such a point is now taken as having no turbulence.
    - [ ] Positivity: horizontally the advection is the tracers' upwind DG, not positivity-preserving at P ≥ 1; a non-positive point is reset to no turbulence by the closure (`turbulence_that_is_not_positive_is_taken_as_none`), and a tiny positive `ψ` still floors `ε` at `ε_min`, a length `k^(3/2)/ε_min` of km (ROMS has the same floor, `gls_Pmin`). A Zhang–Shu scaling of each w-cell level towards its element mean (as the 2D positivity limiter), or a length cap (`l ≤ κ` × the distance to the bed or surface, or the column depth), would make undershoots harmless. Not seen in the gates or `profile_3d`; check on a real run with fronts.
    - [ ] Cost: `turbulence_transport_rhs` staggers a second `LayerTransport` (n_levels + 1 per column, faces included) and copies each field into a scratch to set the end points, every stage. The w-cell fluxes are linear in the layer ones: the transport kernel could read the layer transports and average on the fly (no second transport), and the end-point override could be a boundary condition of the vertical reconstruction instead of a copy.
  - [ ] Cost: `vertical_implicit` 2.1 ms per step with GLS against 0.8 ms with Pacanowski–Philander (1024 P2 elements × 20 levels, 24 threads, `examples/profile_3d.rs mixing=gls`; serial 23 against 8 ms; ≈ 2 % of the step). It is `powf`: `GlsMixing::dissipation`, `psi` and `coefficients` are evaluated two to three times per w-point per step. A k-ε fast path (`ψ = ε`, no `powf`) and one ε per w-point.
  - [x] Surface waves: TKE injection by breaking (Craig & Banner 1994; GOTM's `injection` boundary type) and a Charnock roughness `z₀ₛ = α u*ₛ²/g` instead of the constant 0.02 m. Done 2026-10-01: `GlsMixing::with_wave_breaking(c_w)` and `with_charnock_roughness(α)` (the constant roughness as its minimum, as ROMS's `CHARNOK`). The flux `c_w u*³` makes the model's shear-free layer `k ∝ s^(−a)`, `l = L s` (`GlsMixing::shear_free_layer`: k-ε 4.97 / 0.087, k-ω 2.53 / 0.24, generic 2.00 / 0.19); the `k` and `ψ` fluxes of that layer at the top layer's centre, the `ψ` one blended with the log layer's by how deep the centre lies in the wave-affected layer (≈ 2–4 z₀ₛ).
    - Measured before choosing (2026-10-01): with the log layer `u*` sets only the diagnostic boundary `k`, `ψ`, and from rest the top interior w-point needs ≈ 36 h/u* for 90 % of the log layer's `k` (3 h at 3 m top layers and u* = 1 cm/s, 20 min at 0.3 m). With the shear-free `ψ` condition imposed regardless of depth, a roughness of 2 cm at 0.5 m layers (Charnock's at u* = 1 cm/s) *cut* the top diffusivity 10–100× and the surface current ran to 2.8 m/s (k-ε): the condition shrinks the length inside the log layer. A blend by the local `P/ε` (Burchard 2001's σ_ψ interpolation) fed back on itself (k-ε's mixed layer collapsed after 30 h). The geometric blend has no feedback: with a 2 cm roughness the column is the log layer's (k to 0.6 %); with a wave height's (z₀ₛ = 0.5 m) k-ω and generic hold 4.3–4.4× the log `k` and 2.3–2.9× the diffusivity at the top interior w-point, the mid-depth unchanged, the spin-up one step instead of 15–31 min, and the Kato–Phillips depth unchanged (1.000–1.014 × Price; the entrainment is shear-driven).
    - Gates: `breaking_waves_make_the_shear_free_layer` (still water: `k` to 10 / 7.3 % at 0.1 m layers with k-ω / generic, first order in the layer thickness from the blend; k-ε to 2.7 % at 5 mm), `the_shear_free_layer_balances_both_equations`, `breaking_waves_raise_the_near_surface_mixing_under_wind`, waves in the Kato–Phillips gate.
    - [ ] k-ε with waves: its shear-free layer (`L` ≈ 0.09) is too thin for metre layers, and there it *lowers* the near-surface diffusivity (0.35× the log layer's at z₀ₛ = 0.5 m). Burchard (2001) makes σ_ε a function of `P/ε` (GOTM's `sig_peps`), which gives k-ε a realistic shear-free layer; until then use k-ω or the generic model with waves.
    - [ ] The wave height: Charnock's water-side roughness (cm) leaves the wave-affected layer unresolved at metre layers, so breaking changes nothing there; Terray et al.'s `z₀ₛ ≈ 0.6 H_s` (ROMS `ZOS_HSIG`) needs `H_s`: a reader for MET Norway's wave model (MyWaveWAM, 800 m along the coast) next to `AtmosphereReader`, or a fetch-limited `H_s(U₁₀, fetch)` for fjords. The roughness is one value per model now; per column it would come with `H_s`.
  - [ ] Bed roughness is set twice: `GlsMixing::with_roughness` and `BottomDrag3D::log_layer(z0)` (whose κ is 0.41, the closure's 0.416 for k-ε). Let `Hydrostatic3D::with_bottom_drag` hand its `z₀` to the closure, or take both from one bed description (and the spatially varying `z₀` below).
  - [ ] Thin columns keep their last `k`, `ψ` while thin and start from them when they deepen again; reset them to the minimum (or the neighbours') on re-wetting if shoreline mixing looks wrong.
  - [ ] `PacanowskiPhilanderMixing` keeps its own `g`, `ρ₀` though `Column` now carries the model's; drop the fields (API change). It also still gives unstable, shear-free columns only background mixing (use GLS there).
  - [x] `Solution3D::eddy_viscosity`/`eddy_diffusivity` held `n_levels` values per column, the closures' w-points 0..N−1 labelled as layer centres, without the surface one. Done 2026-10-01: stored at all `n_levels + 1` w-points like `tke` (ROMS's `AKv`/`AKt`); the 3D particle walk reads them.
  - KPP as an alternative: not needed for now.
- [x] Convective adjustment (low-shear unstable columns currently get background mixing). Not needed with GLS: buoyancy production overturns an unstable column within hours (`an_unstable_column_overturns_by_buoyancy_production`). Pacanowski–Philander still has none.
- [x] Per-column surface stress for the 3D model. Done 2026-10-01: `Hydrostatic3D::with_surface_stress(impl SurfaceStress3D)` adds a per-node field `τ(x, t)` to the uniform `Forcing::surface_stress`, in `G` (at `tⁿ`), the vertical diffusion's surface flux and the closure's `u*` (at `tⁿ + Δt/2`). `GriddedAtmosphere2D::split_for_3d` gives the 2D module the pressure gradient and the columns the wind (`GriddedWindStress`, sharing stencils and snapshots); `AnalyticSurfaceStress` takes a function. Gates: `a_varying_wind_reaches_each_columns_drag_balance` (τ varying 3× across a periodic ocean, every column at its own drag balance to 1.1e-13, shear 1.9e-13), `a_time_varying_surface_stress_reaches_the_depth_mean_at_second_order` (rates 1.94, 1.98), `each_column_feels_its_own_surface_stress` (GLS columns bit for bit as lone columns), `gridded_wind_stress_varies_from_node_to_node`.
  - [ ] The surface buoyancy flux is still one domain value (`Forcing::surface_buoyancy_flux`), and the weather readers carry no heat fluxes: a per-column heat/freshwater flux (bulk formulae from `AtmosphereReader` air temperature, humidity, radiation) is the next step for a real 3D run beyond momentum.
  - [ ] Thin columns (`with_min_column_depth`) get no surface stress at all: `G` is zero there and the 2D module carries no wind in a 3D run, so tidal flats under a few dm of water are not wind-driven. Give thin columns the stress through `G` (their depth mean is the 2D module's only, so `τ/ρ₀` alone, without the shear terms).
  - [ ] `GriddedWindStress` is evaluated twice per baroclinic step (`tⁿ` for `G`, `tⁿ + Δt/2` for the columns) into a full `[element][node]` buffer each time; cheap next to the 3D step, but a farm mesh of 10⁶ nodes could keep the `tⁿ⁺¹` evaluation for the next step's `G`.
- [x] Implicit quadratic bottom drag with a log-layer Cd; consistent with 2D friction. Done 2026-09-30 (`physics::bottom_drag`, `Hydrostatic3D::with_bottom_drag`): `τ_b/ρ₀ = C_d|u_b|u_b`, constant or log-layer `C_d` bounded to [0.0025, 0.1]; rate `r = C_d|u_bⁿ|` frozen over the step, `−r·ū` point-implicit in the barotropic pass, `−r·(u_b − ū)` in G, `r·u_bⁿ⁺¹` implicit in the vertical diffusion. Without shear it is the 2D quadratic friction exactly (converges at first order in Δt); wind against drag reaches `C_d u_b² = τ/ρ₀` to 1e-13; the P4.5 beach runs with it.
  - [ ] Second order in time: `r` lags a step (first order, as the 2D point-implicit friction). Re-evaluate `r` from the pass's `ū` per stage, with the frozen shear deviation `u_b − ū` (Thetis-style), if the drag's time error matters.
  - [ ] A spatially varying `z₀` (sediment map, as the 2D roughness-map item in P1.2), and a drag coefficient consistent with the 2D module's Manning n for runs that compare 2D and 3D.
  - [ ] ROMS's `LIMIT_BSTRESS` is not needed (the drag is implicit), but thin columns take `C_d,max = 0.1` from their thin bottom layer: check the wet/dry front speed on a real beach against the 2D `WetDry` with Manning.
