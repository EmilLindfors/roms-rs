### The waves in the viewer (2026-10-20)
Asked for 2026-10-20 ("how we visualize the waves in viz"). Before, the viewer showed none of the wave model: the snapshot files did not carry it, and the photo view's sea came from `--wind` and `--fetch`.
- **The data:** snapshot format version 3 (`io::SnapshotLayout`): *surface fields*, more fields of one value per node between (η, u, v) and the layers; versions 1 and 2 still read (gate `versions_1_and_2_still_read`, `surface_fields_read_back_by_name`). A `froya_real_data wave_coupling= snapshot_minutes=` run writes the sea state there (`waves::SeaStateFields::NAMES`, see `notes/waves.md`). The viewer reads it into `Snapshot::waves` (all seven fields or none), interpolates it in time with the rest, and `--save-snapshot` of a replay keeps it (gate `a_runs_waves_replay_and_save_again`).
- **Colourings** (C cycles speed → η → H_s → T_p → dissipation → speed, the last three only in a run with waves; `--colour hs|tp|dissipation`): H_s on cmocean's thermal, T_p on cividis, whitecapping + breaking on cmocean's ice over four decades (white where the waves lose most). All three monotonic in CAM02-UCS lightness (cv of ΔE 0.06, 0.09, 0.02, sampled as the viewer does). Their scales follow the field (H_s and T_p in round steps to their 99th percentiles, the dissipation's top in decades over its 99.5th; gate `the_dissipation_scale_follows_in_decades`). The wave maps show on opaque water: through the 40 % translucent default the sand of the shallows muddied them.
- **Arrows** under a wave colouring are the waves': along the mean direction of travel, the grid spacing long at the top of the H_s scale.
- **Readout** (hover, pin): "waves H_s 1.12 m, T_p 11.0 s, from 343° (N), spread 54°", and pinned, the power lost to whitecapping and to breaking.
- **The photo view** (`SeaState::with_model`, `ModelSea::around`): 16 of its 32 waves are the model's sea where the camera looks (the variance-weighted T_p, direction and spread of the wet nodes within the view's distance, at least 1 km), a JONSWAP from 0.7 to 6 k_p for H_s 1 m; the shader scales them by the model's H_s per pixel (UV 1 of the water, averaged at shared places as the current is). The wind's 16 start beyond them (its saturation range: roughness, glitter, whitecaps). Whitecaps sit on the wind waves' crests, lifted on the model's crests; surf where the model's breaking takes a share of the energy per period (UV 1's y, `surface::breaking_share`). The waves are rebuilt when the sea where one looks moves on by 12 % in period or 0.12 rad (gates `the_models_sea_is_drawn_for_an_hs_of_one_metre`, `the_models_sea_is_rebuilt_only_when_it_moves_on`).
- Checked by screenshots of the storm replay (`--replay ../output/storm_viz/froya.dgsnap`): `--colour hs --view domain --at 14400`, `--colour dissipation --at 36000`, `--colour tp --at 64800`, and `--photo on --wind 15,250 --vz 1 --eye 3.0,7.7,20,270,4 --at 36000` (from Mausund, westward). The first try of the photo view put whitecaps on the swell's crests, as flat patches 50–100 m across; they are now the wind waves'.
