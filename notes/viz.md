# 3D visualisation, `viz/` (F.3)

### F.3 3D visualisation (`viz/`, Bevy 0.20)
- [x] Crate `dg-viz` in `viz/` (its own workspace, so dg-rs builds and tests never compile Bevy; dg-rs without NetCDF, so no native HDF5). Runs the fjord-farm scenario of `examples/local_time_stepping_farm.rs` on a solver thread (`run_with_callback`), plays the snapshots back (rate, pause, seek; oldest dropped beyond a memory budget) as the water surface over the bed, each element as p² quads of its own nodes with the polynomial's exact normals, coloured by speed or η; walls along the mesh boundary; current arrows sampled by the element polynomial (`Probe2D` weights) on a domain grid and a fine farm grid; net cages (collar, textured net) riding η; orbit camera; `--screenshot` for headless checks (2026-09-27).

  - [x] **The fjord farm in 3D** (asked for 2026-10-07; done 2026-10-07): `--scenario fjord3d`, and "Fjord farm in 3D" in the menu (`Scenario::fjord_farm_3d`, `viz/src/scenario.rs`).
    - The tide: M2 0.8 m through the open boundary as in 2D, into the barotropic mode (`spawn_3d` now takes the 2D scenarios' open boundary, `solver::open_boundary`; Frøya's atlas tides would work the same way). T and S (and the shear) relax to the stratification at rest in a 2 km band along the open face, 30 min on it (`Nesting3D` over `ReferenceColumns`).
    - The stratification (`scenario::fjord_profile`): S 25 at the surface over S 33 sill water, the step at 5 m and 3 m thick, and a summer thermocline, 14 °C over 8 °C at 12 m; salinity sets most of the density step (6 against 1 kg/m³). Surface-stretched σ-levels (Song–Haidvogel 5, 0.4, 10 m, as Frøya's 3D tide). LADiM larvae dive out of the brackish layer.
    - Steep walls: the analytic fjord bed has none inside the domain (r_x0 ≲ 0.05 at 40 m, no flats: 20 m at the shallowest), so `smooth_element_slopes` (pycnocline bound, 20 m free depth) changes nothing there and is a guard for other `--mesh`es; no wetting and drying. The vertical reference is `Simulation3D`'s default; the PGF and the tracer limiter (horizontal Kuzmin, as Frøya's) take the profile as their reference.
    - The rest state (no tide, the open boundary and band in place): with the limiter's reference linear between samples it drifted (4e-5 m/s within 10 min); with `TracerReferenceProfile::from_smooth_fn` (new, P4.6) 3.6e-10 m/s after 1 h (gate `the_stratified_fjord_stays_at_rest`, constant mixing without diffusion; `the_tide_enters_the_3d_fjord` for the tide). With GLS, or a constant 1e-6 m²/s (GLS's background), the halocline diffuses over the slope and drives 3.6e-5 m/s within the hour, the same to three digits, so physics, not GLS numerics: next item.
    - Cost: one global step, set in the farm's quads by the internal-wave bound `(|u| + c₁)/h`, against 8 levels of local time stepping in 2D. Model time per wall time over 15 min of tide (24 threads): the 2D mesh (20 m at the farm, 3584 elements) 9× at P1 × 8 levels, 2.2× at P2 × 12; 40 m (2016) 28× / 7×; 60 m (1488) 48× / 13×. So the 3D fjord runs on its own mesh, `tests/data/gmsh/fjord_farm_3d.msh` (`scripts/gmsh_farm_mesh.py farm=40`), at P1 on 12 levels by default (`--order`, `--sigma` override it).
    - [ ] The diffusion-driven flow at rest under GLS (3.6e-5 m/s at 1 h, still growing). Separate them: halve the levels' spacing and κ in turn, and compare with Phillips's boundary-layer velocity.
    - [ ] Local time stepping in 3D (the mode splitter's step for the whole mesh): the farm's 40 m quads set one step for the fjord's 450 m ones. Even two levels would let the fjord run P2 at the farm.
