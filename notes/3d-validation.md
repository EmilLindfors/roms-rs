# 3D validation (P4.6)

### P4.6 3D validation (before any NorKyst 3D comparison)
- [x] Stratified lake-at-rest, linear N² ≤ 1e-12: the PGF to ≤ 1.5e-14 m/s² over a 30→400 m slope; the mode-split model to 2.8e-11 m/s after 1 h (P4.3 gates, 2026-09-30).
- [x] Stratified seamount at rest (Beckmann & Haidvogel 1993). Done 2026-10-01 (`examples/seamount_3d.rs`), and it found an instability: **a stratified fluid at rest over a slope was unstable**, even with constant N², which the PGF balances to round-off. The round-off velocity grew tenfold every 6 h (16 × 16 P2 elements of 4 km, 20 stretched levels, bed 40–400 m, `r_x0` 0.32; eighteenfold on 2 km elements) and blew up within 3 days, also at `r_x0` 0.08, at any time step, with horizontal viscosity (10 m²/s), vertical viscosity (1e-2), any vertical advection scheme, or a penalty on the layer velocities' face jumps.
  - Cause: the PGF and the density advection did not exchange energy (the PGF's `ρ∇z` must be the negative adjoint of the advection of the stratification along the sloping σ-levels). Growth per 6 h: constant-depth PGF + conservative advection × 11, + split advection × 5; σ-pairs + conservative × 3.5; σ-pairs + split none.
  - Fix: the tracers' horizontal advection in split form (`advective_divergence_element`), the PGF as σ-pairs `(p_j − p_i) + ½(ρ_i + ρ_j)(z_j − z_i)` (`PressureGradientForm::SigmaPairs`, the default; under GLL summation by parts the adjoint of the split form, and with the total potential energy `Σ gρz H_z` exact for any ρ horizontally), and `Hydrostatic3D::with_balanced_reference` to take back the σ form's error at rest (`F_z(ρ_s) − F_σ(ρ_s)` of a reference state added as a fixed field). Gates `a_stratified_seamount_at_rest_stays_at_rest` (7.4e-11 m/s after 36 h; constant depth 8.6e-8 and growing), `a_balanced_reference_gives_the_constant_depth_force_at_rest`. Cost: none (the step 117 → 111 ms in `profile_3d`; σ-pairs need no vertical search).
  - [ ] Tanh pycnocline (3 kg/m³ across 10 m at 30 m, 20 levels): at `r_x0` 0.08, 5 days, the balanced σ-pairs run spins up 0.38 → 1.5 cm/s (σ-pairs alone 4.1, constant depth 4.0 and accelerating). Still growing slowly: run 30 days, refine (nx, levels) to see whether it converges, and compare with Shchepetkin & McWilliams (2003, Fig. 10). At `r_x0` 0.32 it needed horizontal viscosity (Smagorinsky `C_s` 0.5: ≈ 7 cm/s, bounded over 4 days; without, the shear across the pycnocline blew up at 54 h). With the split-form momentum (2026-10-02) it is bounded without (rms 1.5–1.8 cm/s, max 0.2–0.3 m/s over 4 days), and Smagorinsky still lowers the error flow.
  - [ ] The vertical half of the energy exchange is not exact for curved profiles: the PGF integrates the pressure with Hermite-cubic means between levels, the tracers' vertical advection uses limited-Akima face values; both are exact for linear profiles only. Matching them (the face value of the vertical advection as the Hermite mean the pressure uses, or the pressure integrated with the advection's face values) would make the exchange exact for any ρ; check whether it removes the residual growth above.
  - [ ] The balanced reference is computed at the reference state's η: a tide moves the levels by `η(1 + σ)` and the reference's sampling with them (a relative error `η/D`). Refresh it (`set_balanced_reference`) when a parent model's stratification changes, or interpolate the correction to the current level depths; measure with a tide over the seamount.
  - [x] **The horizontal Kuzmin tracer limiter breaks the rest state over a slope**: with `kuzmin=1` the constant-N² seamount reached 0.15 m/s within 15 min and NaN at 2 h (flat bed: round-off). Done 2026-10-01 (`solver::limiters::tracer_3d`), in parts, each found by the one before failing:
    - Bounds at constant height: each node is bounded by its patch's columns of layer means interpolated linearly in `z` to the node's height (extrapolated past the end layers, clamped to the column's nodal range there, so a pit's bed node stays exact and a shoreline film cannot extend a step over metres). A linear `T(z)` is then within its bounds to round-off (unit gate `horizontal_kuzmin_leaves_a_linear_stratification_over_a_bump`).
    - That alone still blew up (2.8e-2 m/s at 2 h): Kuzmin scales the layer's whole deviation from its mean by one α, which over a slope is mostly the stratification along the tilted layer. A round-off clip at a node near the mean height (the P2 centre) set α well below 1 and flattened the layer: growth 2× per 300 s step. Now only the departure from the element's own column at each node's height (`T̂_i`) is scaled, about its inventory-weighted mean `m`.
    - `m` keeps the inventory and must go somewhere when α < 1. As a factor on the column structure (the same ill-conditioning) or as a fallback to the layer mean (it flattened the stratification once perturbations grew: blow-up on day 3), it destabilised the rest state. Now it goes into each node's room within its bounds in proportion, then into the element's widest bounds, then uniformly (without the second tier the P2 lock exchange overshot by 2.4e-9 °C).
    - Curved stratification: layer means cannot represent a pycnocline at the nodes' heights where σ-layers cross it steeply. `TracerLimiter3DConfig::with_reference_profile(TracerReferenceProfile)` limits `T − T_ref(z)` instead; at rest in `T_ref` that leaves 1.5e-5 °C, the profile's sampling error, against 1.8 °C of 17 without (gate `the_kuzmin_limiter_leaves_a_pycnocline_at_rest_with_its_reference_profile`).
      - [x] **The reference's own interpolation error drives a flow at rest under a sharp step.** Found 2026-10-07 with the viewer's 3D fjord (F.3): S 25 over 33 in a 3 m halocline, `from_fn` with 4001 samples over 161 m (4 cm apart) gave 4e-5 m/s within 10 min over the fjord's gentle slopes (r_x0 ≲ 0.05); without the limiter, 9e-11. The error fell 100× per 10× samples (4.8e-7 at 4 mm, 4.2e-9 at 0.4 mm): the limiter sees `T_ref`'s linear error (∝ spacing²) as a departure. Done: `TracerReferenceProfile::from_smooth_fn`, cubic Hermite between the samples with fourth-order slopes of the function (error ∝ spacing⁴; ≈ 10⁻⁸ of the step at 6 cm). The fjord at 2 cm: 3.6e-10 m/s after 1 h (gates `a_smooth_reference_is_exact_for_a_cubic`, `a_smooth_reference_converges_at_fourth_order`; the viewer's `the_stratified_fjord_stays_at_rest`). `from_fn` stays linear (a cubic overshoots at a piecewise-linear profile's kinks).
        - [ ] Move the smooth references to it and measure: Frøya's summer pycnocline without NorKyst (`froya_real_data` `tidal_run_3d` and the `levels=N` timing, `from_fn(…, 4001, profile)`; 4 m thick, so ≈ 40× less curved than the fjord's halocline, but over cliffs), `examples/seamount_3d.rs kuzmin=2`, and the gate at `src/simulation/simulation_3d.rs:3359` (8001 samples over 400 m). Frøya's NorKyst mean profile (`MeanProfile::at`, piecewise linear between its levels) should keep `from_fn`, or sample at its own levels with `new`.
    - 3D-patch bounds: each column also contributes the means of its two layers bracketing the node's height, as Thetis's vertex patches on prisms include the cells above and below. Without them the limiter cut smooth vertical extrema between thick layers (a displaced pycnocline's departure peaks where `∂T/∂z` does): at `r_x0` 0.32 it changed 55–75 % of the element layers every stage by up to 0.2 °C; with them half as many, and the rms error flow after 4 days 7.8e-3 m/s instead of 1.0e-2. The lock exchanges are unchanged (in range to 5e-12 °C).
    - Gates: `a_stratified_seamount_at_rest_stays_at_rest_under_the_kuzmin_limiter` (constant N², 36 h: 7.9e-11 m/s, 7.2e-11 without the limiter); the lock exchanges stay in range to 2–5e-12 °C, their fronts within 0.005 in Fr (P1 0.483 → 0.488: the bounds now follow `η`). `examples/seamount_3d.rs` (16 × 16 P2, 20 levels): constant N², `kuzmin=1`: 1.1e-10 m/s at 3 days (the same without the limiter), 3.6e-10 at 10 days. Tanh pycnocline at `r_x0` 0.08, balanced, `kuzmin=2` (the initial profile as reference): 5.1e-3 / 1.05e-2 m/s at 2 / 5 days, no limiter 7.0e-3 / 1.5e-2; `kuzmin=1` NaN by 24 h.
    - [ ] At `r_x0` 0.32 (balanced tanh, Smagorinsky `C_s` 0.5) the limiter still adds to the error flow: rms 7.8e-3 m/s after 4 days against 6.1e-3 without it (the flow is eddying: compare rms, not the noisy maximum). The exact solution is rest; at this `r_x0` the σ-pairs PGF is ≈ 30× off for departures from the balanced pycnocline, so every non-advective change of T over the flanks is a spurious force, while the advection exchanges energy with the PGF consistently (P4.6).
      - Literature (2026-10-01): Marchesiello et al. (2009, Ocean Modelling 26): any dissipation along σ over slopes is spurious diapycnal mixing, and "the accurate application of strictly monotonic schemes to the sigma-coordinates remains an unsolved and largely unacknowledged issue"; remedies are dissipating only a departure from a reference (Mellor et al. 1998: our `TracerReferenceProfile`) or dissipation rotated to geopotentials (RSUP3; the limiter's reset target `T̂_i` is at constant height). Lemarié et al. (2012, Ocean Modelling 42): only isoneutral dissipation damps the error growth over steep topography. Thetis (Kärnä et al. 2018): Kuzmin's vertex limiter in 3D on prisms (the 3D-patch bounds above). Aizinger et al. (2017, IJNMF 84): anisotropic limiting of each gradient component.
      - Tried and not kept: Kuzmin's (2010) hierarchical limiting at P2 (the departure's linear part and curvature limited separately, the curvature by the patch's mean gradients): rms 8.1e-3 alone, 1.1e-2 with the 3D-patch bounds; with Kuzmin's rule α₁ = max(α₁, α₂) the P1 lock exchange overshoots 2 °C (a front over two elements has a smooth gradient), without it the P2 one 2.6e-7 °C. Troubled-cell limiting (only layers whose patch has a face jump of the departure > θ × the bounds' spread, after Krivodonova et al. 2004): the best seamount yet (rms 4.2e-3 at θ = 0.3, 5.4e-3 at 0.1, both below no limiter), but the lock exchanges overshoot 0.003–2 °C at either θ: the overshoots grow where the field is steep but continuous, so face jumps do not find them. Wider bounds (`relax=3`): 7.3e-3.
      - Candidates: a detector that sees steep continuous fronts as well as jumps (e.g. the jump of the gradient, or the ratio of the highest modal coefficients, Persson & Peraire 2006) tuned against both the seamount and the lock exchanges; with T and S both stratifying, bounds and the reset target along isopycnals for the spice.
    - [ ] A source for the reference profile: a horizontal-mean profile of a state (binned in `z`) or of the parent model's fields (`OceanModelColumns`), refreshed as the nesting's stratification changes. Only `TracerReferenceProfile::new`/`from_fn` exist; a realistic run with fronts and a pycnocline needs one before it uses this limiter over slopes.
    - [ ] Periodic meshes: `Mesh2D::elements_at_vertex` has no periodic images, so a vertex on a periodic boundary has half its patch (a corner of a fully periodic mesh: one element). Kuzmin bounds there come from one side only; this affects the 2D limiters too (`limiters/swe_2d.rs:260`, `tracer_2d.rs:593`). Found with the seamount's corners.
