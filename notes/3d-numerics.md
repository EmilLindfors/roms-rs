### P4.3 Pressure gradient and vertical grid
- [x] Balanced PGF. Lift pressure/η jumps at element faces. Done 2026-09-30 (`solver::rhs::baroclinic`): ∇p|_z directly, as the DG derivative of pairwise pressure differences at a common depth (Stelling & van Kester 1994 in a flux-differencing form), each column's ρ a monotone Hermite cubic in z integrated exactly (the Shchepetkin & McWilliams 2003 ingredient), one-sided pairs where a node lies below the other column's bed, and the pressure jump at every face lifted with a central flux.
  - Constant N² at rest over a 30→400 m slope: round-off (≤ 1.5e-14 m/s²) at P1–P4, uniform and stretched levels; the σ form gave 1.3e-5 – 2.5e-3.
  - The review's pycnocline fjord on 30 stretched levels (θs 5): 2.0e-7 / 1.5e-6 / 3.3e-6 m/s² at P1 500 m / P3 250 m / P3 100 m, against 1.6e-3 / 1.1e-4 / 2.2e-5 for the σ form and ≈ 5e-5 of real estuarine forcing.
  - Mode-split fjord at rest (10→150 m over ≈ 400 m, linear N²): 2.8e-11 m/s after 1 h; on `main` it blew up within 16 steps.
  - Since 2026-10-01 (P4.6, the seamount) the default form is σ-pairs, consistent in energy with the split-form tracer advection; the constant-depth form above stays as `PressureGradientForm::ConstantDepth`, and its balance at rest is reached by σ-pairs with a balanced reference state. σ-pairs alone on the pycnocline fjord: 6.8e-4 / 1.5e-4 / 1.7e-5 m/s².
  - [ ] On 30 *uniform* levels the pycnocline case stays at 2–4e-4 m/s² (σ form 4e-4 – 1.6e-3): 13 m apart at 400 m, the deep columns do not sample a 3 m pycnocline. Resolve it in every column (stretched levels, as NorKyst), or subtract a finely tabulated horizontal-mean reference profile ρ̄(z) before integrating (Mellor et al. 1998) — an option to add if real stratification needs it.
  - [ ] Second order for smooth fields at P2 (measured 1.7–1.8 in the max norm, pre-asymptotic); first order in the one-sided pairs at the bottom of steep slopes. Higher-order vertical reconstruction (unlimited slopes where monotone) is the lever if needed.
  - [x] Cost: n_nodes²/2 pair evaluations per level per element (81/2 pairs at P2). Done 2026-10-01: parallel over elements, and each pair evaluated at all levels in one upward walk of both columns (no binary search) with the own-level pressures tabulated, bit-identical; 1.8 ms per call at 1024 P2 elements × 20 levels on 24 threads (serial before: 45 ms). Next lever if it shows: the cubic integrals vectorised across levels (structure of arrays per pair).
  - [ ] Curvilinear elements: the pairs use the affine metric (`Hydrostatic3D` asserts affine); the collocated chain rule `rx_i Dr + sx_i Ds` extends it.
- [ ] rx0/rx1 diagnostics and ROMS-style bathymetry smoothing. r_x0 is done in 2D (`Bathymetry2D::max_rx0`, volume-preserving `smooth_rx0`, 2026-09-29, P1.3). Still open: r_x1 (Haney number), which needs the σ levels, and an r_x1 bound for the 3D grid.
- [ ] Vtransform 2 + the true Vstretching 4. `ROMSVstretching4` is not ROMS Vs4, and `hc` is unused; Ω must use ∇·(Hz·u) once Hz varies horizontally.

### P4.5 Remaining 3D physics and numerics
- [x] Higher-order vertical advection. Done 2026-09-30 (`VerticalAdvection`, `Hydrostatic3D::with_vertical_advection`): fourth-order Akima (ROMS `TS_A4VADVECTION`) is the default for the tracers, and upwind remains an option. The mode-1 internal seiche on 10 levels: upwind 1.06 % slow, 1.2 % amplitude loss per period; Akima 0.37 %, 0.14 %, second order in the level spacing (gates `akima_vertical_advection_keeps_internal_waves`, `a_mode_1_internal_seiche_has_the_internal_wave_speed`).
  - [x] Akima is not monotone. Done 2026-09-30: `VerticalAdvection::Tvd`, third-order upwind with a TVD limiter (Koren 1993; the spatial part of HSIMT, Wu & Zhu 2010, whose Courant-number terms the SSP-RK3 stages replace). A front advected 16 layers: TVD stays in [0, 1] over 6 layers, upwind smears it over 18, Akima overshoots 16 %; on a tanh halocline TVD is 60× more accurate than upwind, Akima 800× (`vertical_schemes_on_a_front_and_a_smooth_profile`). Out of an end layer it is linear through the two end layers (first order there froze the bed layer at a wall, and a seiche over a thick bed layer drifted by a third of its amplitude per period).
    - The beach's 1.0e-3 °C undershoot was not a defect of Akima's: the layer means start linear in z, so the water at the bed is 0.26 °C colder than the bed layer's mean, and rising, it cools the layer (TVD: 1.6e-3 °C). Upwind stayed in range only because it freezes the bed layer at a wall.
  - [x] Akima in index space. Done 2026-09-30: Akima and TVD reconstruct on the layers' σ-thicknesses (`LayerTransport::d_sigma`), exact for linear profiles on any spacing. On Song–Haidvogel θs 5, θb 0.4 levels the divergence error of layer means of a smooth profile at 160 levels: index-space Akima 9.2e-3 (stalling), thickness-aware 3.2e-4; TVD 9.7e-4 against 3.8e-6 (third order).
    - The internal seiche on stretched levels is no judge of it: its sampled sine is not the discrete mode on uneven layers, and the index-space form's period error is smaller there at 10–40 levels (+0.23 % against +0.33 % on 20), both converging to −0.03 % at 80.
  - [x] The momentum's vertical advection. Measured 2026-09-30, and it stays second-order centred (`VerticalAdvection::Centred`, linear and non-dissipative); `Hydrostatic3D::with_momentum_vertical_advection` selects Akima. All 3D gates pass with either.
